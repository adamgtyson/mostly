# MOSTLY — Game Design Document

## Document 5: Monastic Orders

-----

## Overview

Each outer region has one monastic order. All orders are named **The [Badass-Sounding Noun]** — serious and weighty on the surface, which makes whatever is actually happening inside them funnier by contrast.

The critical thematic note: **every monastic order is accidentally describing The Forge’s maintenance requirements through their theology.** They have been living adjacent to The Forge’s influence for so long that its operating requirements seeped into their cosmology. They are all, unknowingly, monks of The Forge.

Patch will figure this out before they do. Patch will not be gentle about telling them.

-----

## THE GRINDING

*Monastic order of The Turning — the region containing The Forge*

**Settlement:** The Abbey of the Hopefully Infinite Thrum
*Named for the sacred sound made by properly balanced Spooky Fluid — "the Thrum."*

The monks are perpetually drunk on their own beer and/or high on their own cultivation. They are the most important people in the world. They are absolutely blasted at all times. This is not dereliction. This is coping. They are devoted. They just also happen to be very impaired.

The sober one: every monastery has one. Probably young, probably anxious, probably the only person who fully understands what happened and has been trying to tell the others for weeks. Has been waiting for someone to actually show up and ask.

**Two sounds, never connected (locked):** The order's own name, The Grinding, comes from a different sound entirely — the noise The Forge makes when Spooky Fluid runs low (see Document 4). The Thrum (contented, properly balanced) and the Grinding (a maintenance warning) are two physically distinct sounds. The monks venerate one and dread the other without ever consciously connecting them to the same machine. A notable, deliberate irony — not a throwaway detail.

**What The Grinding accidentally describes about The Forge:** The grinding sound is a maintenance warning. It is not holy. It is a maintenance alert. Nobody filed the paperwork.

## THE ORDER OF THE MOST SACRED MYSTERY

*Primary order of The Turning; the order that directly tends The Forge*

**Official Motto:** “Probably.”
**Older Motto:** “We Shouldn’t Rule It Out.”
*(The current motto represents genuine theological progress over the older one.)*

**Monastic Greeting:**

> “Are you well?”
> “As far as anyone knows.”

### Core Belief

The founders discovered empirically that every novice who confidently explained the Forge became haunted almost immediately. The novices who answered “I’m…not actually sure” lived long, peaceful lives. This observation became theology. Their entire belief system is evidence-based. They arrived at intellectual humility through empirical observation of supernatural consequences.

They are accidentally the most rigorous scientific institution in the world, operating as a monastery, slightly drunk, next to a floating gyroscope they’ve agreed not to fully explain.

### Sacred Virtues

- Reasonable doubt
- Careful observation
- Saying “I don’t know”
- Taking another look
- Reading the footnotes

### The Great Heresy

Absolute certainty. Every schism in the order has ended when both sides eventually admitted: “Perhaps we were getting ahead of ourselves.”

### Daily Ritual: The Litany of Unfinished Questions

> “Will the sun rise?”
> “Probably.”
> 
> “Will the Forge continue to turn?”
> “Probably.”
> 
> “Will Brother Edwin finally finish cataloguing the east archive?”
> *(long silence)*
> “…probably not.”

### Sacred Relics

**The Empty Box**
No one knows what’s inside. Opening it is forbidden — not because it is dangerous, but because the monks agree that not knowing is currently more useful. Patch will want to open it immediately. The monks will explain why that would be a waste. Patch will find this simultaneously stupid and completely reasonable.

**The Unlabeled Key**
Definitely opens something. Finding out can wait.

**The Fifth Appendix**
An ancient manuscript references a Fifth Appendix. No copy has ever been found. The annual search is mostly an excuse for a picnic.

### Named Monks

**Brother Edwin** — the order's perpetually-behind archivist; the punchline of the Litany of Unfinished Questions (see above).

**Brother Aldous** — the order's memory for precedent. Introduced to Patch via the summons letter (Document 2). Always suspects, never confirms, that the Fifth Appendix holds the answer to whatever crisis is at hand.

### Holy Festivals

**The Feast of Plausible Outcomes**
Citizens gather to make predictions. Closest prediction wins. Nobody is allowed to claim certainty. Children love it. Statisticians become celebrities.

**The Day of Tentative Conclusions**
The monks spend twelve hours debating profound theological questions. At sunset the official ruling is published: *“Further study is warranted.”* Celebrations follow.

### Patch’s Relationship with This Order

Patch arrives expecting answers. The Order of the Most Sacred Mystery has tended The Forge for generations. They know more about it than anyone alive. Their answer to every question Patch asks will be some variation of “probably” and “we’re not certain” and “that’s an excellent question that warrants further study.” And they will be right. Every hedged, uncertain, carefully qualified thing they tell Patch will turn out to be accurate. While every confident claim made by every other institution in the world turns out to be wrong. Patch will find this profoundly irritating and then, eventually, deeply respectful. They will not say the second part out loud.

## THE BROTHERHOOD OF PROPER MARGINS

*Monastic order of The Measure*

**Core Belief:** “Precision is kindness.”

Their sacred texts all have exactly one-inch margins. Every copied manuscript is measured before being blessed. The heresy of writing too close to the edge implies a schism — probably multiple schisms — over margin width. Someone died on this hill. Possibly literally.

**Settlements:** Linecross, Half Chain, Square Field, Plumb
*(Every street in Linecross is perfectly straight. Population of Half Chain is measured every Tuesday. Square Field is astonishingly rectangular. No building in Plumb has ever leaned.)*

**What they accidentally describe about The Forge:** Tolerances. Operating parameters. Acceptable variance. “Precision is kindness” is the maintenance philosophy of a machine that runs on exact measurements.

-----

## THE MONASTERY OF RESPECTFUL SEPARATION

*Monastic order of The Distance*

**Core Belief:** Everything should have enough room to exist comfortably. Ghosts become aggressive when crowded. People probably do too.

**Ritual:** Before greeting someone, both participants take one polite step backward.

Patch will find this deeply irritating and will do it anyway because it’s actually reasonable.

**Settlements:** Far Enough, Personal Space, Across, One More Field
*(All famous for extremely wide roads.)*

**What they accidentally describe about The Forge:** Safe distances. The Department of Acceptable Distances in Margin maintains laws about distance from “anything that hums.” This order developed the same theology independently, from proximity to the hum.

-----

## THE SILENT CUSTODIANS OF AMBIENT NOISE

*Monastic order of The Quiet*

**Core Belief:** Silence is not the absence of sound. It is proper sound management.

**Daily Routine:** Monks walk the forests listening. If something sounds wrong, they write it down.

**Sacred Instrument:** The Listening Stick.
*(It is just a stick.)*

Do not ever explain the Listening Stick further. Do not give it powers. It is just a stick. That is why it works.

**Settlements:** Hush, Moss Bell, Whisper End, Lowbranch
*(Every tavern serves remarkably quiet soup.)*

*Note on the soup: it implies the soup has acoustic properties, which implies someone measured them, which implies the Brotherhood of Proper Margins was involved at some point.*

**What they accidentally describe about The Forge:** The Grinding sound. The hum. Monitoring ambient noise for wrongness is exactly what you’d develop as a theology if you lived near a machine that broadcasts maintenance warnings as sound.

-----

## THE ABBEY OF CONTINUOUS DRAINAGE

*Monastic order of The Flow*

**Core Belief:** Everything should flow. Water. Air. People. Paperwork.

**Greatest Sin:** Standing water.

**Festival:** The Great Cleaning of Ditches. The entire province participates. Tourists are confused.

**Settlements:** Bucketford, Little Lock, Spill, Mill Again, Wet Corner

**What they accidentally describe about The Forge:** Fluid flow. Specifically, Spooky Fluid flow. The greatest sin of standing water is the theological expression of a machine that breaks when its fluid stops moving properly.

-----

## THE ORDER OF COUNTERWEIGHTS

*Monastic order of The Balance*

**Core Belief:** For every stone moved, another should feel appreciated. Nobody understands this. The monks do.

**Ritual:** Every evening they place one small rock somewhere else. Reality remains remarkably stable.

**Settlements:** Stack, Evenhill, Counterstone, Lean
*(Every building looks like it should fall over. None ever have.)*

**What they accidentally describe about The Forge:** Rotational balance. Counterforce. The Forge is a gyroscope — its stability depends on balanced opposing forces. This order has been maintaining something equivalent through rock placement for generations without knowing it.

-----

## Thematic Summary

All six orders are unknowing monks of The Forge:

|Order                |Theology                |Forge Function They Describe            |
|---------------------|------------------------|----------------------------------------|
|Most Sacred Mystery  |Epistemic humility      |Not overclaiming the machine’s operation|
|Proper Margins       |Precision is kindness   |Tolerance specifications                |
|Respectful Separation|Everything needs room   |Safe operating distances                |
|Silent Custodians    |Sound management        |Maintenance warning monitoring          |
|Continuous Drainage  |Everything must flow    |Spooky Fluid circulation                |
|Counterweights       |Balance for every action|Rotational equilibrium                  |
